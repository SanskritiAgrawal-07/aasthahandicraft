Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Crafted with Devotion

Aastha Handicrafts was founded by the Agrawal family — Damodar Agrawal, Alok Agrawal and Indresh Agrawal — with a single vision: to carry the rich heritage of Rajasthani craftsmanship into every home celebrating Raksha Bandhan.

From our workshop in Lalsot, Dausa, we work hand-in-hand with local artisans, preserving traditional techniques while crafting designs that modern hearts adore. Every thread, bead and stone reflects their unwavering dedication to quality — because for us, every Rakhi is not just a product, it is a promise.
